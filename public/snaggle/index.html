Download videos from X

Snaggle is a free Chrome extension that saves videos from X (Twitter) straight to your computer — in the quality you pick.

Add to Chrome — free

Works in Chrome, Edge, Brave and Opera. No account, no sign-up, no limits.

How to download a video from X

  1. Install Snaggle and open x.com as usual.
  2. Find any post with a video. Snaggle adds a download icon to that post's action bar, right beside reply, repost and like.
  3. Click it. A menu lists the qualities available for that video.
  4. Pick one. The MP4 saves to your normal Downloads folder while a progress card tracks it in the corner of the page.

Pick your quality

Most downloaders decide for you. Snaggle lists every resolution X actually offers for that video — commonly 1080p, 720p, 360p and 270p — with an estimated file size next to each one. Take the full-quality MP4, or a smaller file to save space and data.

Or use the toolbar

Opened a post in its own tab? Click the Snaggle icon in your browser toolbar and download it from there instead. The popup speaks seven languages and matches whichever X theme you use.

Why Snaggle

  • Genuinely free. No account, no sign-up, no monthly download cap, no ads and no premium tier.
  • You choose the quality, and you see the file size before you download.
  • Real progress. A live percentage from your browser's own download, not a spinner that tells you nothing.
  • Blends into X. Snaggle detects which of X's three themes you use — Default, Dim or Lights out — and restyles itself to match, instantly, with no page reload.
  • Seven languages. English, German, French, Italian, Spanish, Portuguese and Ukrainian, following your browser automatically.
  • Nothing collected. No analytics, no telemetry, no tracking code.

Privacy, briefly

Snaggle collects nothing about you. It asks x.com for the video files of the post you clicked using the session you are already signed in with, so it only ever sees what you can already see yourself. It makes one small request to our own server to look up a technical identifier that X rotates periodically — that request carries nothing about you, your account, or what you downloaded.

The only things stored are your chosen language and your X theme colour, both on your own device. Full details in the privacy policy.

Questions

Is Snaggle free?

Yes, completely. There is no account, no sign-up, no monthly download limit, no ads and no premium tier. Every feature is available to everyone.

Do I need to sign in to anything?

Not to Snaggle — it never asks for a password and has no account system. You do need to be signed in to X in your browser, because that is what lets you view the video in the first place.

Where do the downloaded videos go?

Straight to your browser's normal Downloads folder, saved as an MP4 by Chrome's own download function. The file never passes through any server of ours.

Can I choose the video quality?

Yes. Snaggle lists every resolution X offers for that video — commonly 1080p, 720p, 360p and 270p — with an estimated file size next to each, so you can take full quality or a smaller file to save space and data.

Why does it ask for access to api.gmeffect.com?

X periodically rotates an internal identifier that is required to request a video's files. Snaggle makes one small request to our own server to look up the current value. That request has no body and carries nothing about you, your account, the post you are viewing, or what you downloaded.

Does Snaggle collect my data?

No. There is no analytics, no telemetry and no tracking code of any kind. The only things stored are your chosen language and your X theme colour, both kept on your own device.

Does it work in browsers other than Chrome?

Yes. Chromium-based browsers such as Microsoft Edge, Brave and Opera can install it directly from the Chrome Web Store.
