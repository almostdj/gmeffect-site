Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 August 2026

This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the Green Mouse Effect website at gmeffect.com and our browser extensions. We are an independent software studio, and we have kept this deliberately short and specific.

In short: we do not sell your data, we do not run advertising or analytics, and we do not build profiles of you. Our browser extensions send us nothing about you or what you download.

The website

Contact form

If you send us a message, the name, email address and message text you type are submitted through Web3Forms, which forwards them to our mailbox at contact@gmeffect.com. We use that information only to reply to you. We keep the message in our mailbox for as long as needed to handle the conversation, and you can ask us to delete it at any time.

Hosting

The site is hosted on Cloudflare Pages. Like any web host, Cloudflare processes technical connection data such as your IP address and browser user-agent in order to serve the pages and protect against abuse. We do not have access to an analytics dashboard for this site and we do not track individual visitors.

Fonts

The site loads typefaces from Google Fonts. Your browser therefore requests those font files from Google's servers, which reveals your IP address to Google as part of that request. No cookies are set by us for this, and we receive no data from it.

Cookies

We do not set cookies for advertising, analytics or tracking on this site.

Browser extensions

This section covers Snaggle, our video downloader for X (Twitter), and applies to any future extension we publish unless its listing says otherwise.

What the extension does not do

  • It does not create an account, and it never asks for a password.
  • It does not send us your identity, your username, or your IP address.
  • It does not report which posts you view or which videos you download.
  • It contains no analytics, telemetry, advertising or tracking code.
  • It does not sell or share any data, because it collects none.

What is stored on your own computer

The extension saves two small settings locally in your browser, using Chrome's own extension storage. They stay on your device and are never transmitted to us:

  • the interface language you chose;
  • the background colour of your X theme, so the extension can match it.

What the extension connects to

Snaggle contacts exactly two hosts:

  • x.com — to look up the video files of the post you clicked. This request uses the session you are already signed in with in your own browser, so it can only ever see content you could already see yourself. It goes directly from your browser to X, and we never see it.
  • Our lookup endpoint — X periodically changes an internal identifier that is required to request a video. The extension asks our server for the current value. This request contains no information about you: no account, no username, no post, no download history, and no request body of any kind. Our server returns a single identifier and stores nothing about the caller beyond the ordinary, short-lived connection logs that any web server keeps.

Downloaded videos are saved straight to your computer by your browser's own download function. The files never pass through us.

Permissions

The extension requests only the permissions it needs to do the above: saving downloads, storing the two settings named earlier, detecting whether the current tab is an X post, and access to x.com so it can add its button to the page.

Your rights

If you are in the EU, EEA or UK, data protection law gives you the right to ask what personal data we hold about you, to have it corrected or erased, and to object to how we use it. In practice, the only personal data we are likely to hold is a message you sent us through the contact form. Write to us and we will act on your request.

Children

Our site and extensions are not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes we will update the date at the top of this page. Material changes to how we handle personal data will also be noted in the relevant extension's store listing.

Contact

Questions about this policy, or a request about your data: contact@gmeffect.com.
